About Me

I'm an Information Technology graduate with over five years of hands-on experience supporting business-critical systems — from Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central administration to POS operations and sales data reconciliation. That work put me close to how real software actually gets used: I've spent years troubleshooting system issues, maintaining master data, and making sure transaction records stay accurate across accounting, finance, and IT teams.

That experience is what's pulling me toward backend development. I've seen firsthand how much a well-built system matters — and how painful a poorly built one is to support. So I'm now building that skillset directly: Python, FastAPI, REST API design, SQL, and Git, with a growing focus on writing the kind of backend code I used to depend on from the other side.

I enjoy the problem-solving side of this most — figuring out why something breaks, designing a clean data model, or getting an API endpoint to behave exactly the way it should. I'm not claiming to be a senior engineer. I'm a developer with real operational experience who is actively and seriously building toward a backend role, one project at a time.
